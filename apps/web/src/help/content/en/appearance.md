# Appearance

Nautix has a dark theme (black, with white buttons) and a light one (white, with black buttons). Pick one, or follow your system, from the **Theme** menu in the top bar. The accent color and glass options are in **Settings, Appearance**.

![Settings, Appearance: accent colors and the transparency option](img:appearance)

## Accent color

The accent is a fine detail: the selected node, links, zones, keyboard focus and small badges. Buttons do not use it.

- Pick one of the presets or any color with **Custom**. **Reset** goes back to the default.
- Red, green and yellow are not offered. They mean error, ok and warning everywhere else, so an accent in those colors would be confused with a state. A custom color in those ranges is moved to the nearest allowed one.
- Nautix checks the contrast against the background and adjusts the lightness when needed, in both themes. The line under the swatches says what it did.

## Glass

Only the layers that float have a glass look: the top bar, the inspector, messages and dialogs. In Chrome, Edge and Brave the edge also bends what is behind it, like a lens. Everything else is solid.

Turn on **Reduce transparency** for solid panels without blur. It switches on by itself when your system asks for less transparency. With **Reduce motion** on your system the dialogs also open without animation.

The settings are saved in this browser only.

The default is **Aurora**: blue that fades into teal, like the logo. The second color is the accent's hue shifted toward teal (never red, green or yellow); it colors the overlay boxes and tunnels, and together with the accent it forms a gradient on the selected node, the active tab dot, the top edge of the inspector and a soft glow behind the header. Pick another accent and the pair follows.
